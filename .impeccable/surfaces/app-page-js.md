# Surface brief — homepage (`app/page.js`)

Mode: **Persuade**. Client-facing demand funnel. Visitor: a person or business in India hiring
private security, on a phone as often as a laptop, buying safety from strangers. Job: understand
what 20fourr is, believe verification is enforced by the software, and pick a city and book.
Proof on hand: real client-app screenshots, the duty ticket, the badge vocabulary, PSARA citations,
the published cancellation clock. No invented stats, reviews or testimonials.

Constraints: PRODUCT.md brand commitments (ink ground, paper bands, amber sole accent, green for
verified only, Big Shoulders / IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Mono, mono button labels). All copy
preserved; legal/compliance wording untouched.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category canon, played straight at Linear/Ramp/Mercury finish — pinned by the user,
who named those five products as the bar, so it beats the concept roll. It refuses the
"thirteen equal bands, each restarting with eyebrow + H2 + lede" arrangement the incumbent ships.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ink ground in three layered depths, 1px hairlines with a faint top-edge
highlight, small radii (6px controls, 12px panels), amber used only for the primary action, the
live state and the one emphasised word; green only where something is verified. Light uppercase
Big Shoulders for display at a steep scale jump, Plex Sans for everything read, Plex Mono for codes,
figures and button labels. One paper band, reserved for the limits.

STORY: Visitor sees the offer and the booking control in one viewport, sees a closed duty record
as proof, walks the real booking screens one at a time, learns exactly what is checked, then what
is not promised, then what happens if it goes wrong, then books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7/12: H1 (~4 lines, up to 4.5rem), sub, city select + amber "Book verified
security", verify link, fine print. Right 5/12: the duty ticket as a raised product panel with a
soft amber glow behind it, rows lighting in sequence. Credential strip pinned to the fold line.

FORM: Canon SaaS landing (user-pinned; no seed roll). Signature interaction: the booking-flow
step picker — a numbered step list beside a single phone stage that swaps screenshots, keyboard
and click driven; collapses to the swipe carousel on mobile. Motion grammar: one orchestrated
ticket sequence, crossfades on the stage, transform-only rises from a visible default.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
