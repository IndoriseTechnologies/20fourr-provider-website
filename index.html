How a booking runs

From request to release of payment.

Nine states, one direction. Nothing skips a step — and money only moves when the step before it is proven.

  1. 01
    You send a request

    Pick a category, dates, times and the address. Tell us the purpose — wedding, industrial site, travel, a specific threat — so we route it to someone who has done it before.

  2. 02
    The provider accepts

    They see the job and either take it or decline with a reason. No silent expiry, no waiting to find out.

  3. 03
    You pay — and details unlock

    Only now do you get the guard's phone number and address, and only now do they see your threat brief. Before payment, both sides stay private.

  4. 04
    You issue the start code

    A six-digit code appears on your phone. You read it out when they arrive. They enter it. That is the arrival record — and it releases the first 30%.

  5. 05
    You issue the end code

    Same again at the end of the shift. The duty closes, you both rate each other, and the remaining 70% is scheduled.

Trust & compliance

The rules are in the software, not in a policy document.

A promise you have to enforce by hand is not a protection. These are constraints the platform applies on every single booking, whether or not anyone is watching.

Contact privacy

Numbers stay hidden until you pay

A provider cannot see your address and you cannot see their number until the booking is paid. It keeps deals on the platform — which is what keeps the licence checks, the insurance trail and the dispute route intact.

Threat brief

They arrive knowing what they're walking into

If you've been threatened or attacked before, you record it once in your profile. It's released to the assigned provider after payment only — late enough to protect you, early enough for them to prepare.

Verification

KYC reviewed by a person

Documents are uploaded, then approved or rejected with a written reason by our compliance team. Unverified providers cannot accept a single booking. Gunmen need a current firearm licence on top.

Cancellation

Refunds on a published clock

Cancel more than 24 hours out and 90% comes back to your wallet. Between 12 and 24 hours, 50%. Inside 12 hours, nothing — because by then the guard has already turned down other work.

Disputes

A ticket, an owner, an outcome

Raise a dispute and it becomes a tracked ticket with an assigned reviewer, a full chat record, and a resolution type — refund, credit, replacement or penalty. Not an inbox.

Ratings

Both directions

You rate the guard on professionalism, punctuality, communication and compliance. They rate you too. Ratings follow the account, so repeat behaviour is visible before anyone accepts.

Two kinds of credit, deliberately kept apart

SecureCoins

What a cancellation refund comes back as. ₹1 each, no cap, applied to your total like cash.

SecurePoints

Earned from referrals and loyalty. Applied before tax, so they cut your GST too — up to 20% of a booking.

They are not interchangeable, and that is on purpose: one is a discount in the eyes of the tax law, the other is a way to pay. Merging them would quietly misstate what you owe.

Questions

Before you book.

What happens if the guard doesn't show up?

Report it in the app and the penalty is calculated from how close to the start time it happened. Inside two hours, the provider forfeits the entire booking value, is suspended for fifteen days and has their PSARA privileges blocked. A support case is opened for you at the same time — you don't have to chase anyone.

Can I meet or verify the guard before the shift?

You can see their profile, ratings, experience, badges and verification status before booking. Their phone number and address unlock once payment is made, and you can message them in the app from that point until the booking closes.

Is the armed guard's firearm licence actually checked?

Yes, and not just once. The provider uploads it during KYC and it is verified by our compliance team, then re-confirmed at the point the booking is assigned. A gunman booking cannot proceed without a current verified licence on file.

How do I get my money back if I cancel?

Refunds go to your SecureCoins balance, usually within 24 hours: 90% if you cancel more than 24 hours before the start, 50% between 12 and 24 hours, nothing inside 12 hours. Coins are worth ₹1 each and can be spent on any future booking with no cap.

Do you give a GST invoice I can claim?

Every booking generates an itemised tax invoice the moment payment is captured. If your account is registered as a business with a GSTIN, the invoice is addressed to it and states the applicable regime — forward charge or reverse charge — explicitly.

I run a security agency. Can I put my whole team on this?

Yes. Register as a firm, complete the agency KYC once, then assign staff per booking with their credentials attached. Registered firms have TCS withheld at 1% and appear in our monthly GSTR-8 filing, so your tax position is documented rather than reconstructed later.
